PART 2 TEMPLATE

 

  • I’m really into + noun/V-ing, so I decided to join this club to connect with people who share the same interests as me.
  • I’m passionate about/I’m keen on  + topic, because it helps me + benefit. Moreover, I can + extension idea.

Example:

  • Sports: I’m really into playing badminton, so I decided to join this club to connect with people who share the same interests as me.
  • Books: I’m passionate about reading books because it helps me expand my knowledge and improve my concentration skills.
  • Garden: I’m keen on gardening, so I want to meet people who enjoy planting trees and sharing gardening experiences.
  •  

PART 3 TEMPLATE

TEMPLATE 1: Opinion

  • In my opinion, + opinion.
  • Moreover, + reason.
  • As a result, + positive/negative effect.

Example: In my opinion, technology has made communication much easier. Moreover, people can connect with others around the world instantly. As a result, building relationships has become more convenient than before.

TEMPLATE 2: Experience

  • I usually/often + activity.
  • Additionally/Sometimes, + second activity.
  • Overall, + benefit/feeling.

Example: I usually read books before going to sleep. Additionally, I sometimes visit libraries at weekends to find new novels. Overall, reading helps me relax and expand my knowledge.

TEMPLATE 3: Advice

  • I think + suggestion.
  • It will help somebody + benefit.
  • Moreover, + second benefit.

Example: I think taking a course would be a great idea. It will help you improve your skills more effectively. Moreover, you can receive guidance from experienced teachers.

PART 4

- INFORMAL EMAIL TEMPLATE

Hi Tony,

I’ve just read the club’s latest email, and I feel really excited/disappointed about the situation. Personally, I think that + opinion/suggestion. It would be a great opportunity/problem for members. What do you think about it?

Best,

Mary

- FORMAL EMAIL TEMPLATE

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing in response to your email regarding + topic. I was very excited/disappointed to hear this news because + reason.

In my opinion, + main opinion/problem. Moreover, + explanation/example. I believe that this issue may affect members in several ways.

To resolve this issue, I would recommend that the club should + suggestion. This would help members + benefit. In addition, + second benefit/result.

I strongly believe that these changes would improve the overall experience for members and make the club more appealing.

I look forward to hearing your response.

Yours sincerely,

Mary

TEMPLATE: Suggesting a Place

  • In terms of location, I would suggest + place because + reason.
  • It is + adjective + and + adjective.
  • Moreover, + additional benefit.

Example: In terms of location, I would suggest organizing the event at a coffee shop in the city centre because it is convenient and easily accessible. Moreover, the atmosphere is comfortable and suitable for members of different ages.

Convenient: easily accessible, located in the city centre, convenient for members

Comfortable: relaxing atmosphere, comfortable environment

Suitable: suitable for all age groups, ideal venue

Transportation: easy to travel to, close to public transport

TEMPLATE: Suggesting a Guest Speaker/Celebrity

  • I would recommend inviting + person/job title because + reason.
  • Not only is he/she + quality, but he/she also + second quality.
  • I believe that his/her appearance would + result.

Example: I would recommend inviting a well-known entrepreneur because not only is he highly experienced, but he is also an inspiring public speaker. I believe that his appearance would attract more participants and motivate young people.

Influencer: has a strong influence on young people

Chef: has extensive cooking experience

Artist: is well known for creativity

Businessperson: has practical business knowledge

Athlete: inspires people to live healthily

TEMPLATE: Giving Advice/Suggestions

  • To address this issue, I would recommend that + subject + should + verb.
  • This would help + somebody + benefit.
  • Moreover, + second benefit.

Example: To address this issue, I would recommend that the club should organize more outdoor activities. This would help members relax and connect with one another more easily. Moreover, it could attract more young participants.

  • In my opinion, the best solution would be to + verb.
  • By doing this, + positive result.

Example: In my opinion, the best solution would be to create official accounts for all members. By doing this, the club could reduce negative comments and improve communication between members.

  • I think it would be a good idea to + verb because + reason.

Example: I think it would be a good idea to separate contestants into different age groups because it would make the competition fairer.

  • How are you? I’m fine thanks.

  • What’s your favorite season? I really like autumn.
  • What’s your favorite animal? I adore dolphins.
  • How many people are in your family? There are four of us.

Part 2:  

Please tell us why you are interested in food. Use 20-30 words.

My grandmother used to do the cooking, and I loved helping her in the kitchen. I also enjoy traveling, and I’m really into trying food from different countries.

 

Part 3: You are in the “Food Club” chat room. Answer the questions from other members. Use 30-40 words

  • Hi, and welcome to the club! What types of food do you like cooking? 
  • I really enjoy trying to follow my grandmother’s recipes. She’s Nigerian and makes amazing dishes using yams and plantain. Recently I’ve also been experimenting with herbs and species and different kinds of sauces.
  •  
  • What’s the best restaurant you’ve been to? What was it like? 
  •  
  • I’d have to say The Black Swan, a curry house in Bradford. The decoration makes you feel as if you’re really in India, and they specialize in South Indian dishes. The Goan fish dish is to die for!
  •  
  • What's the worst restaurant you’ve ever been to? What was it like? 
  • It was The Friendly Fish in Blackpool. The service was absolutely appalling. I had to wait 25 minutes for my meal. Then when it finally arrived, the chips were cold and greasy and the fish was frozen in the middle.

Part 4:

Dear Member

We are writing to inform you that the TV chef Jay Goffery won’t be able to attend the club’s annual meeting due to an administrative error. Unfortunately he has been double-booked on the 19th May. We suggest changing the date of the meeting to the following week, so that he can join us as planned. Please let me know whether this arrangement would be satisfactory to you.

Regards

Tyrone O’Neal

 

Write an email to your friend. Write about your feelings and what you think the “Food Club” should do about the situation. Write 40-50 words.

 

Hi Dave,

I’ve just read the email from Tyrone and I’m a bit annoyed. I’m not keen on putting off the meeting, as I won’t be around on the 26th. I think they should stick to the date, or look for a different chef. What do you reckon?

Cheers,

John

 

Write an email to the president of the club. Write about your feelings and what you think “Food Club” should do about the situation. Write 120-150 words.

 

Dear Mr. O’Neal,

I am writing to express my real disappointment at hearing the news that Jay Goffery will not be able to attend our annual meeting on the 19th. I am afraid I do not find your proposed solution acceptable, and I will explain why. If the meeting is postponed to the following week I will not be able to attend, as I will already have left for my holiday.

Therefore I would like to put forward an alternative suggestion. In my opinion, you should write to Jay to ask him to cancel his other engagement and honor our original arrangement. If I remember correctly, he confirmed this date with us nearly a year ago. If he refuses, perhaps we should consider approaching another celebrity chef.

I look forward to hearing your reply.

Yours sincerely,

John Harrop
