Form viết email

Cập nhật lúc 05:35 - 27/08/2026

Writing : 50 Minutes

Part 1: 5 điểm

Part 2 : 7 điểm

Part 3 : 13 – 15 điểm

Part 4 : 20 – 25 điểm

Part 2,3 : 

 

Part 4 : Email cho bạn : 50 – 75 từ

Mỗi phần chọn 1 trong các câu để dựng thành 1 khung bài cho mình nhé.

1.    Chào hỏi

Hi Tim/Hi there Tim/Morning Tim/ Hello again Tim

2.    Hỏi thăm

How have you been ?/How’s everything going? How are you these days?/How are you doing?

Long time no see/ We haven’t talked to each other for a while…

I hope you’re doing well./ I hope all is well with you/ I hope everything is going well for you…

3.    Dẫn dắt vào lý do viết thư

  • -    I just got an email from the ….. Club. They said that…..

-    I have some exciting/interesting/bad… news for you. My ….club is seeking suggestions for…..

-    I am writing to tell you some good/bad/interesting....news/. My ….club ….

-    I have heard that my club…..

-    I just received an email from my..........club manager. In the email, he said that my ….club…..

4.    Cảm xúc

I feel so happy/sad/disappointed/excited to hear that.

5.    Giải pháp.

I think the club should……../I believe we should…/I feel that we should…/I suggest that we…/We should consider…

(Phần này phải tự nghĩ ra giải pháp cho yêu cầu của đề bài)

6.    Hỏi ý kiến bạn

Do you agree with my suggestions/ideas/opinions/decisions…?

What’s your opinion about this? Could you give me advice or some ideas? What should I do?....

7.    Kết thư

Please write to me soon and let me know what you think.

Please write back to me as quickly as possible

Bye for now

Speak soon

Kí tên

Formal Letter to manager : Thư cho quản lý : 120 – 250 từ

1. Chào hỏi

Dear Sir/Madam/Mr..../Mrs./Manager/ .....Club President/Manager

2.    Giới thiệu

I hope you're having a great day/ I hope this email finds you well/I hope this email reaches you in good health and high spirits.

My name is...I have been a book/music/food....club member since 2020/last summer/for two years/more than two years.

3.    Lý do viết thư

-    I’m writing in response to the email you sent. 

-    I'm writing to respond to your email mentioning that…..

-    I’m so upset/ disappointed/ excited to hear that...

-    I am writing to express my feelings about the notice you sent me  stating that….

-    As a dedicated member of the club for two years, I am writing to share my thoughts and suggestions regarding the recent notice of the club. According to the news,...

-    As a dedicated member of the club for three months, I am writing to express my feelings and suggestions about the recent notice we received. The issue of….

- I saw your email about/asking for/stating/saying that….+ trích đề

I understand that…+trích đề

4.    Trích lại nội dung chính của đề

-    According to the news/notice/email.....

-    In the email, you said that.....

-       In the email you asked me to...... => chuyển nội dung đề bài xuống một cách hợp lý, đổi ngôi phù hợp

5.   Giải pháp, đề xuất, lựa chọn, ý kiến....tùy đề yêu cầu

I think/In my opinion, we should….

I suggest we  + V infinitive

 I recommend we V infinitive

Do you think the club can try rescheduling the trip/the meeting.....

It would be great if.....

6.    Câu chốt cho đủ từ nếu bài còn ngắn

-    I have spoken to other club members, and they share my thoughts

-    I have talked to other club members and found that they share my opinions.

All of us have been waiting for this for a long time (nếu là sự kiện vui vẻ của CLB)

7.    Kết thư

Please let me know what you think/if this is possible

I hope my suggestions/ideas...are helpful.

Thank you for your attention

I am looking forward to hearing from you.

I appreciate your attention to this matter.

Please feel free to reach out if you need more information

Yours faithfully,

Regards,/ Warm regards/All the best/Respectfully/Kind regards
