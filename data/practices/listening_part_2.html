Câu 1/16
Four people are talking about Reading. Complete the sentences below.
Speaker A

Person A : Reads while travelling to work.

 

It was probably my favorite activity when I was younger. I used to spend hours reading novels and storybooks at home. These days, I'm quite busy with work, so I don't have much time to sit down and read. However, I still manage to enjoy a few pages every morning while travelling to work by bus. It's a great way to make use of my journey.

Speaker B

Person B : Does not have time to read books.

 

When I was a kid, I really enjoyed reading adventure stories and comics. I even had a large collection of books in my bedroom. However, my life has become much busier since I started working. I usually spend my free time doing housework or meeting friends, so I hardly ever have enough time to read books nowadays.

Speaker C

Person C : Prefers another activity to reading.

 

My house is full of books because I've been collecting them for many years. I particularly enjoy buying novels and keeping them on my bookshelves. Although I sometimes read a few pages before going to bed, I actually prefer watching films and listening to music. These activities help me relax much more than reading does.

Speaker D

Person D : Only reads children's books.

 

As I was small, my parents often bought me picture books and fairy tales. I still enjoy reading, but my interests haven't changed very much. Even though my friends recommend exciting novels and science books, I don't find them interesting. I prefer children's books because they have simple language, lovely pictures and interesting stories.
